## Summary

The Cardano Foundation has introduced the 2026 edition of its annual State of the Cardano Developer Ecosystem Survey through a governance **Info Action**.

Conducted annually since 2022, the survey seeks feedback from developers, technical stake pool operators and other ecosystem contributors about their experiences, development tools, infrastructure requirements and challenges.

The 2026 edition introduces on-chain publication of questions and responses using **CIP-179**, enabling independent verification and analysis of the collected information.

The survey is available in nine languages, takes approximately 15 minutes to complete and does not require participants to hold ADA or connect a wallet.

The governance action is intended to raise awareness and encourage participation. It does not request Treasury funding, introduce a protocol change or seek approval for a substantive governance decision.

RCADA welcomes the initiative as a useful contribution to ecosystem understanding and responsible, evidence-informed governance.

The recorded **ABSTAIN** position reflects the informational nature of the action and is consistent with the proposer's stated intention.

## Key Considerations

- **Ecosystem insight:** The survey provides an opportunity to identify developer experiences, tooling challenges, documentation gaps and infrastructure priorities.

- **SPO participation:** Technical stake pool operators are specifically invited to contribute their perspectives on operating and maintaining Cardano infrastructure.

- **Transparency and reproducibility:** CIP-179 enables survey questions and responses to be recorded on-chain and independently examined.

- **Accessibility:** The survey supports nine languages and requires neither a wallet nor ADA to participate.

- **Privacy considerations:** Responses, including free-text submissions, are publicly recorded on-chain. Participants should understand the permanence and accessibility of their contributions.

- **Representative limitations:** Survey findings should be interpreted in the context of participation levels, self-selection and potential response bias.

- **Governance purpose:** This is an informational initiative intended to encourage participation rather than obtain approval for a particular expenditure or policy.

## What This Action Does

The Info Action publicises the 2026 State of the Cardano Developer Ecosystem Survey and encourages greater participation from the technical community.

The survey gathers feedback concerning:

- Developer backgrounds and experience.
- Programming languages, tools and libraries.
- Development infrastructure and services.
- Documentation and educational resources.
- Technical challenges and barriers to development.
- Stake pool operations and infrastructure experiences.
- Emerging technologies and development priorities.
- Areas where ecosystem support or investment may be beneficial.

## Analysis Findings

### Constitutional / Governance Assessment

This Info Action does not seek to withdraw Treasury funds or change Cardano protocol parameters.

Its primary purpose is to communicate information and encourage community participation.

The action includes publicly accessible supporting information, an on-chain metadata reference and links to the survey and its open-source implementation.

No material constitutional objection has been identified in this review.

**Assessment:** Informational governance action with no direct Treasury expenditure or protocol-change risk.

### Transparency and Data Considerations

The introduction of CIP-179 provides an opportunity to demonstrate verifiable surveys as a practical Cardano application.

Publishing survey data on-chain allows independent parties to examine the responses, reproduce analyses and assess the findings without relying solely on a centrally published report.

This is a constructive use of blockchain infrastructure.

However, transparency also introduces privacy considerations.

Responses are publicly accessible and potentially permanent, including information supplied through free-text fields.

Participants should avoid including confidential information, sensitive operational details or unnecessary personally identifying information.

The ability to independently verify responses also does not establish that the respondents represent the entire Cardano developer population.

### Ecosystem Impact

Developer surveys can support more informed decisions about ecosystem priorities.

In particular, the results may help:

- Identify persistent tooling and documentation problems.
- Highlight infrastructure requirements.
- Inform future development initiatives.
- Support more evidence-based Treasury assessments.
- Improve understanding of technical SPO experiences.
- Track changes in the ecosystem over time.

The 2025 survey received 109 responses. While such feedback is useful, sample size and respondent selection must be considered before drawing broad conclusions.

The 2026 initiative could strengthen the evidence available to builders, funders and governance participants, provided its findings are reported transparently and interpreted with appropriate context.

**Assessment:** Constructive ecosystem research initiative, with public-response privacy and survey representativeness as the principal considerations.

## RCADA Rationale

RCADA welcomes the 2026 State of the Cardano Developer Ecosystem Survey as a constructive initiative to better understand the experiences, challenges and priorities of developers and technical stake pool operators across Cardano.

I believe that gaining a clearer understanding of the people building and maintaining our ecosystem is essential to its long-term development. Reliable feedback can help identify where improvements are needed in developer tooling, documentation, infrastructure and community support, while informing more effective governance and Treasury decisions.

RCADA particularly welcomes the survey's multilingual accessibility, open-source implementation and use of CIP-179 to make the collected information independently verifiable.

I also appreciate the effort to make participation straightforward and accessible without requiring a wallet or ADA.

One important consideration is that survey responses, including free-text submissions, are published permanently on-chain. Participants should be fully aware of these implications and take appropriate care when sharing personal, professional or potentially sensitive information.

While survey results can provide valuable insights, I would also encourage their interpretation in the context of participation levels and potential response bias. A survey is most useful when its findings are presented transparently, including any limitations in how representative the responses may be.

As this is an informational initiative rather than a request for Treasury funding, a protocol change or a substantive governance decision, RCADA records an **ABSTAIN** position, consistent with the proposer's stated intention.

This abstention does not indicate opposition. On the contrary, RCADA supports initiatives that improve ecosystem understanding, promote informed decision-making and encourage constructive participation.

RCADA encourages developers, technical SPOs and other eligible contributors to participate and share their experiences.

This approach aligns with RCADA's commitment to responsible governance, decentralisation and the **generation and regeneration of environmental, social and economic sustainability**.
